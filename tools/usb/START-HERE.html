This stick turns any screen into a living race: cars racing real-layout circuits under floodlights, close battles from the chase cam, champagne on the podium. Pick the way that fits your screen.

Easiest · any smart TV

Straight into the TV

  1. Plug this stick into the TV's USB port.
  2. Open the TV's media or USB app and go into TV - plug the stick into the TV.
  3. Play the first video and turn on Repeat all (or Loop) in the player's options.

Live · never repeats

With a computer

  1. Connect a computer or mini PC to the screen and plug in this stick.
  2. Open Computer - Windows, Mac, Linux, then your system's folder, and start Lapwall.
  3. It opens full screen in wall display mode: a new race every few minutes, floodlights that follow the time of day, and the screen kept awake.

Windows: if SmartScreen appears, choose More info → Run anyway. Mac: unzip, then right-click Lapwall → Open the first time. Linux: allow the AppImage to run as a program.

Lock it, leave it

Wall display mode hides the cursor and ignores the keyboard, so nobody passing by can change it. To leave, press Ctrl Shift L (Cmd Shift L on a Mac) and enter your PIN.

Set a PIN from the Lapwall icon next to the clock (the menu bar on a Mac): Wall display → Set a PIN. You can also choose which screen it uses and whether it starts on its own when the computer turns on.
